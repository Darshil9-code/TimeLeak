# TimeLeak ⏳

**TimeLeak** is a minimalist, mobile-first web application that maps your lifespan into a glowing grid of 4,160 weeks. It visualizes how passive daily digital habits (like social media, gaming, or streaming) quietly consume your remaining years, and gives you actionable tools to take that time back.

## Features

- **Lifespan Grid:** Enter your date of birth to see your exact week-by-week life progress, color-coded by lived weeks, remaining weeks, and time wasted on habits.
- **Habit Cost Calculator:** Calculate the true lifetime cost of habits like Instagram or gaming in hours, reels watched, or lost value.
- **Reclaim & Streaks:** Set daily limits to cut back, build streaks through daily check-ins, and earn back green dots on your life grid.
- **WhatsApp Sharing:** Generate custom high-resolution status cards or shareable text summaries to keep yourself accountable.

## Built With

- Pure HTML, CSS, and JavaScript (Vanilla JS)
- HTML5 Canvas for real-time glowing dot animations
- LocalStorage for client-side state management
